About Luna Apparel

Luna Apparel was established in 2024 with the goal of making fashionable clothing accessible and affordable. Our collection includes carefully selected pieces for everyday outfits, special occasions and everything in between.

Our Mission

To provide fashionable and affordable clothing while delivering excellent customer service.

Our Vision

To become one of South Africa's leading fashion retailers, recognised for quality, affordability and customer satisfaction.

Our Values

  • Quality
  • Affordability
  • Customer satisfaction
  • Style
  • Accessibility

Our Customers

Luna Apparel is aimed at customers who want fashionable, affordable and versatile clothing for everyday wear and special occasions.
